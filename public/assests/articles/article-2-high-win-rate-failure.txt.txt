--- ARTICLE CONTENT ---

A common misconception among beginner traders is that a high win rate guarantees profitability. In financial markets, win rate is only one half of an equation. Without proper risk-to-reward calibration, an 80% win rate strategy can lead to total capital exhaustion.

THE HIGH WIN RATE ILLUSION:
- 8 WINNING TRADES : +₹1,000 each = +₹8,000 Gross
- 2 LOSING TRADES  : -₹5,000 each = -₹10,000 Gross
--------------------------------------------------
- GROSS P&L        : -₹2,000 Loss
- EXECUTION FEES   : -₹1,200 (10 trades round-trip)
- FINAL NET P&L    : -₹3,200 NET LOSS

The Expectancy Formula

Mathematical expectancy defines the average amount a trader can expect to win (or lose) per rupee risked over a sample size of trades:
E = (W * R_net) - ((1 - W) * 1)

Where:
- E = Mathematical Expectancy per trade (in R-multiples)
- W = Win Rate (expressed as a decimal)
- R_net = Net Risk-to-Reward Ratio (Net Reward / Net Risk)

If E > 0, the trading system possesses a positive statistical edge. If E < 0, the system will deplete capital over time regardless of how frequently individual trades win.

Comparing Three Trading Strategies:

Strategy A (High Win Rate):
- Win Rate: 80% (0.80)
- Net R:R: 1:0.25
- Avg Win: ₹250
- Avg Loss: ₹1,000
- Expectancy per Trade: -₹0.00 (Break-Even)
- Net P&L over 100 Trades: ₹0 (Minus Fees = Net Loss)

Strategy B (Balanced):
- Win Rate: 50% (0.50)
- Net R:R: 1:1.50
- Avg Win: ₹1,500
- Avg Loss: ₹1,000
- Expectancy per Trade: +₹250 (Positive Edge)
- Net P&L over 100 Trades: +₹25,000 Profit

Strategy C (Asymmetric R:R):
- Win Rate: 30% (0.30)
- Net R:R: 1:3.00
- Avg Win: ₹3,000
- Avg Loss: ₹1,000
- Expectancy per Trade: +₹200 (Positive Edge)
- Net P&L over 100 Trades: +₹20,000 Profit

The Break-Even Win Rate Matrix:
Break-Even Win Rate (%) = (1 / (1 + R_net)) * 100

- Net R:R 1:0.5 -> Required Win Rate: 66.67%
- Net R:R 1:1.0 -> Required Win Rate: 50.00%
- Net R:R 1:1.5 -> Required Win Rate: 40.00%
- Net R:R 1:2.0 -> Required Win Rate: 33.33%
- Net R:R 1:3.0 -> Required Win Rate: 25.00%

--- FAQ SECTION ---

Q: Why do traders naturally gravitate toward high win rate strategies?
A: Cognitive psychology leads human brains to equate winning frequency with competence. Accepting small, frequent wins feels satisfying, but avoiding stop-losses often leads traders to hold losing positions too long, causing large drawdowns that erase multiple gains.

Q: What is a sustainable net risk-to-reward ratio for day trading?
A: A net ratio between 1:1.5 and 1:2.5 (after accounting for execution charges) provides a sustainable balance for day trading. It allows profitability even if win rates fluctuate between 40% and 50%.
